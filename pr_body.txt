🚨 Severity: HIGH
💡 Vulnerability: Multiple auxiliary Pydantic models (e.g. MCPServerConfig, RetrievalEvent, ToolCall) were missing the reject_newlines validator on identifier fields.
🎯 Impact: Newline injection into identifiers could bypass downstream validation, leading to log injection, command injection, or path traversal.
🔧 Fix: Applied the reject_newlines field validator to all identifier fields across all Pydantic models, and ensured model_config = ConfigDict(validate_assignment=True) is set.
✅ Verification: Verified via pytest that newlines in identifiers now raise ValueErrors during model initialization.
